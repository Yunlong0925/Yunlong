## About Me
I am a first-year PhD student at the University of St Andrews, supervised by Prof. Jonathan Fraser and Dr. Natalia Jurga. My research focuses on **dimension theory** and **complex dynamics**, exploring fractal dimensions in dynamical systems.

**Research Interests**:
- Fractal geometry
- Complex iteration theory
- Hausdorff dimension analysis
- Multifractal formalism
